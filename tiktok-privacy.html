Privacy Policy (TikTok)

Last updated: 2026-11-16

"liam-ai-dev-app" ("the app") is a personal tool that publishes short animated videos to the owner's own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

What we access

The app uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API only with the owner's own TikTok account. It reads the basic profile (display name and avatar) to confirm which account is connected, and it uploads the owner's own videos with their titles, captions and hashtags.

What we collect and store

We do not collect personal data from visitors or viewers. The app stores only the owner's access and refresh tokens and the post metadata, on the owner's own computer. We do not sell, share or use any data for advertising, and we do not give it to third parties.

Data from TikTok

Likes, comments and views on a post stay on TikTok under TikTok's own policies. The app does not collect data about other TikTok users.

Revoking access and deletion

You can remove the app at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy > Security and permissions > Apps and services, then select "liam-ai-dev-app" and remove it. This revokes its tokens. You can also email us and we will delete any data linked to you within 30 days.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-16

"liam-ai-dev-app" ("the app") is a personal tool that publishes short animated videos to the owner's own YouTube, Facebook and Instagram accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

What we access

The app uses the Meta Graph API only with the owner's own Facebook Page and Instagram professional account, to upload videos, titles, captions and hashtags, and to read the performance of those posts (views, likes, comments).

What we collect and store

We do not collect personal data from visitors or viewers. The app stores only the owner's access tokens and the post metadata on the owner's own computer. We do not sell, share or use any data for advertising.

Data from Meta

If you interact with a post (like, comment), that data stays on Facebook or Instagram under their policies. The app may read public comment text on the owner's posts for analytics. It is not shared with anyone.
